WHY IT MATTERS

Benefits are how you compete
for the people who matter.

In Ohio's current labor market, competitive benefits are one of the most powerful tools an employer has for attracting and retaining quality employees. The cost of turnover — recruiting, training, lost productivity — almost always exceeds the cost of a better benefits package.

As an independent broker, Myers Insurance Group shops the Ohio market to find group benefits programs that maximize value for both employer and employee. We aren't tied to any single carrier, which means we find the right fit rather than the easiest sale.

We also help you manage the administrative burden — open enrollment, compliance, employee communication, and carrier relations — so you can focus on running your business.

What Ohio employers often get wrong

Renewing with the same carrier every year. Most employers renew automatically and never shop the market. Rates increase. Coverage options improve elsewhere. An annual review almost always finds better options.

Undervaluing voluntary benefits. Dental, vision, and disability coverage can often be offered at little to no cost to the employer — but employees value them significantly.

Missing compliance requirements. Ohio employers face specific ACA compliance requirements based on size and structure. We help you stay compliant without hiring a dedicated HR specialist.
