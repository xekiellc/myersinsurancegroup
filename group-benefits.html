Group Life Insurance

If something happens to one of your employees, their family should not have to post a GoFundMe. Group life insurance is one of the most meaningful — and most affordable — benefits you can offer.

  • Term life coverage for employees
  • Typically 1–2x annual salary benefit
  • Low employer cost per employee
  • Optional employee-paid supplemental coverage
  • AD&D (accidental death & dismemberment) add-on

Group Disability Insurance

Short-term and long-term disability insurance replaces a portion of an employee's income if they're unable to work due to illness or injury. It protects your employees' most valuable asset — their income.

  • Short-term disability coverage
  • Long-term disability coverage
  • Typically 60–70% income replacement
  • Employer and employee-paid options
