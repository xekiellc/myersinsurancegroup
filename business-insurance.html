WHAT WE COVER

Core business coverages.

Every business is different. Your coverage should be too. Here are the core products we build business policies around.

General Liability

Protects your business from third-party claims of bodily injury, property damage, and personal injury. The foundation of any business policy.

Commercial Property

Covers your building, equipment, inventory, and contents against fire, theft, vandalism, and other covered perils.

Commercial Auto

Vehicles used for business need commercial coverage — personal auto policies won't cover business use. We cover fleets, single vehicles, and everything in between.

Workers Compensation

Required in Ohio for most businesses with employees. Covers medical expenses and lost wages for employees injured on the job.

Business Owner Policy (BOP)

Bundles general liability and commercial property into one cost-effective policy. Ideal for small to mid-size businesses.

Professional Liability

Also called Errors & Omissions (E&O). Protects service-based businesses against claims of negligence, mistakes, or failure to deliver.

Liquor Liability

Essential for bars, restaurants, and any business that serves alcohol. Covers claims arising from alcohol-related incidents.

Commercial Umbrella

Adds an extra layer of liability protection above your primary policies. Critical for businesses with significant exposure.

Cyber Liability

Covers data breaches, cyberattacks, and the costs of recovery. Increasingly essential for any business that stores customer data.
