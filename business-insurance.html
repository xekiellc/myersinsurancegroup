WHAT WE COVER

Business coverage built
for Ohio operations.

A complete business insurance program covers more than just GL. Here's what Ohio businesses actually need.

General Liability

Covers third-party injury, property damage, and basic business claims. The foundation of every business policy.

Workers Compensation

Required in Ohio for most employers. Covers employee injuries on the job — non-negotiable for any business with staff.

Commercial Property

Covers your building, equipment, inventory, and build-out against fire, theft, vandalism, and covered perils.

Professional Liability

Covers claims from your professional services. GL doesn't cover this — E&O is a separate and essential policy.

Commercial Auto

Covers vehicles used for business. Personal auto policies exclude business use — one accident without this is a serious problem.

Liquor Liability

Required if you serve alcohol. Ohio dram shop laws make you liable for patron behavior. One incident without this can close your business.

Business Interruption

Covers lost revenue when a covered loss forces you to close temporarily. Most businesses that close after a disaster never reopen without it.

Cyber Liability

Covers data breaches, ransomware, and notification costs. Standard policies exclude cyber claims entirely.

Commercial Umbrella

Adds excess liability protection above your primary coverages. Essential for any business with significant liability exposure.
