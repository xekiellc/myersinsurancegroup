WHAT WE COVER

Gym and fitness coverage in Ohio.

A complete Ohio fitness business insurance program covers your facility, your instruction, your equipment, and your members.

General Liability

Covers member injury, slip-and-fall, and property damage claims on your premises. High-traffic fitness environments with heavy equipment create constant premises liability exposure.

Professional Liability

Covers claims from fitness instruction — a member injured following your programming, a client who claims your advice caused harm, or a training protocol that leads to injury. Essential for any fitness professional.

Commercial Property

Covers your gym equipment, free weights, machines, flooring, sound system, and build-out. Fitness equipment is expensive — proper valuation is critical for any gym.

Equipment Breakdown

Covers repair and replacement when your treadmills, cardio equipment, and HVAC systems fail. Standard property policies exclude mechanical breakdown — this coverage is essential for equipment-heavy gyms.

Workers Compensation

Required in Ohio for employees. Fitness instructors, personal trainers, and gym staff face real physical injury risk. Non-negotiable for any fitness business with employed staff.

Cyber Liability

Member databases, payment information, and health data all create cyber exposure. A breach affecting member health or payment data carries significant notification and legal costs.

Business Interruption

Covers lost membership revenue when a fire or covered loss forces you to close temporarily. Monthly membership revenue disappearing during repairs can quickly become a permanent closure.

Employment Practices Liability

Covers harassment and wrongful termination claims from employees and instructors. Fitness environments with close physical contact between staff and members face elevated EPLI exposure.

Commercial Umbrella

Adds excess liability above your primary coverages. Serious member injuries — spinal injuries, cardiac events, equipment accidents — can generate claims that exceed standard policy limits.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Gym and fitness insurance in Ohio — what you need to know.

Do member waivers protect me from liability claims?

Partially — but not completely. Ohio courts have upheld gym liability waivers in many cases, but waivers don't protect you from claims of gross negligence, equipment that wasn't properly maintained, or injuries to minors. A waiver reduces your risk — it doesn't eliminate it. General liability and professional liability coverage remain essential regardless of what your membership agreement says.

I'm an independent personal trainer — do I need my own insurance?

Yes. The gym's policy covers the gym — not you or your instruction. As an independent trainer, you carry your own professional liability exposure for every session you conduct. Your own professional liability policy protects you against claims from clients regardless of whose facility you're training in. Many gyms now require independent trainers to carry their own coverage as a condition of using the facility.

A member had a cardiac event during a class — are we covered?

This depends on the circumstances and how the claim is characterized. If the claim involves premises liability — an AED that wasn't maintained, a delay in calling emergency services — GL responds. If the claim involves professional negligence — a class that was too intense for the member's disclosed fitness level, or failure to properly screen a new member — professional liability is involved. Having both coverages is essential for any fitness facility.

My treadmills broke down during peak season — is that covered?

Only with equipment breakdown coverage — which standard property policies specifically exclude. Equipment breakdown coverage pays to repair or replace your cardio and strength equipment when it fails due to mechanical or electrical breakdown. For a gym whose members pay for access to functional equipment, a breakdown event without this coverage means full out-of-pocket repair costs and potential membership cancellations.
