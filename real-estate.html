The one thing most landlords find out too late

Your homeowner's policy does not cover a rental property. Not even close. The moment you put a tenant in that house, your personal homeowner's policy no longer applies — and if something goes wrong, you're exposed.

One bad tenant. One fire. One slip and fall. One busted pipe in a 12-unit building at 2am. That's all it takes.

The question isn't if something goes wrong. It's whether you have the right coverage when it does.
