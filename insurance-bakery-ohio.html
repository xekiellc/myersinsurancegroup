WHY BAKERY INSURANCE IS DIFFERENT

Bakeries face product liability exposure every single day.

Bakeries are food production businesses — and food production businesses carry product liability exposure that retail stores don't face. Every item you sell is a product that someone will consume. Allergen exposure, contamination, and food-borne illness are real and insurable risks that standard General Liability policies often limit or exclude.

Beyond product liability, bakeries depend heavily on commercial equipment. A commercial oven failure during your busiest week doesn't just mean lost production — it means lost orders, lost revenue, and potentially lost customers. Equipment breakdown coverage is essential for any bakery where the oven going down means the business goes down.

Custom order bakeries — wedding cakes, corporate orders, seasonal specialties — also face professional liability exposure when a key order doesn't meet expectations. Myers Insurance Group builds Ohio bakery policies that address all of it.

Coverage gaps that hurt Ohio bakeries

Allergen exposure is a product liability claim. A customer with a nut allergy who has a reaction to your product has a product liability claim. Standard GL policies often limit or exclude food product liability — dedicated product liability coverage is essential.

Equipment breakdown isn't covered by property insurance. When your commercial oven or mixer fails, standard property coverage doesn't pay for the repair. Equipment breakdown is a separate coverage that specifically addresses mechanical failure.

Custom order disputes are professional liability. A wedding cake that's the wrong flavor, the wrong design, or delivered late is a professional liability matter. GL won't cover it.

Cottage bakery operations need coverage too. Home-based Ohio bakeries operating under Ohio's cottage food laws still have product liability exposure — and standard homeowner's policies exclude business operations.

WHAT WE COVER

Bakery coverage in Ohio.

A complete Ohio bakery insurance program covers your production, your products, your equipment, and your customers.

General Liability

Covers slip-and-fall, customer injury, and property damage in your bakery. The foundation of any bakery policy — and required by most commercial landlords and farmers market operators.

Product Liability

Covers claims from food-borne illness, allergen exposure, and contaminated product. Every item you sell is a product liability exposure. One allergen incident without this coverage can generate a claim that exceeds your annual revenue.

Commercial Property

Covers your bakery build-out, display cases, retail area, non-equipment contents, and signage against fire, theft, vandalism, and covered perils.

Equipment Breakdown

Covers repair and replacement when your commercial ovens, mixers, proofers, and refrigeration units fail. Standard property policies exclude mechanical breakdown — this coverage is essential for any production bakery.

Business Interruption

Covers lost revenue and ongoing fixed costs when a covered loss — fire, equipment failure, or property damage — forces you to stop production. For bakeries with regular wholesale accounts or event commitments, BI coverage is critical.

Professional Liability

Covers custom order disputes — wedding cakes, corporate orders, event baking. If a custom order doesn't meet the client's expectations and they sue, professional liability covers your legal defense and any resulting claim.

Workers Compensation

Required in Ohio for employees. Bakery work involves burns, cuts, heavy lifting, and repetitive motion — all common injury sources. Workers Comp is non-negotiable for any Ohio bakery with staff.

Commercial Auto

Required if you deliver orders. Personal auto policies exclude commercial delivery use — catering deliveries, wedding cake transport, and wholesale delivery runs all need commercial auto coverage.

Cottage Bakery Coverage

Ohio cottage food law allows home-based bakery operations — but your homeowner's policy excludes business operations. Dedicated cottage bakery coverage protects home-based bakers against product liability and business liability claims.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Bakery insurance in Ohio — what you need to know.

A customer had an allergic reaction to something I baked — am I covered?

This is a product liability claim — and it's one of the most serious exposures for any food business. Standard GL policies often limit or exclude food product liability. Dedicated product liability coverage is what responds to allergen exposure, contamination, and food-borne illness claims. Without it, you're personally liable for the full cost of the customer's medical expenses, lost wages, and legal costs — which can be substantial for a serious allergic reaction.

My commercial oven broke down the week before Christmas — is that covered?

Only if you have equipment breakdown coverage — which standard property policies specifically exclude. Equipment breakdown coverage pays to repair or replace your commercial oven, mixer, or other production equipment when it fails due to mechanical or electrical breakdown. For a bakery, the oven going down is a business-stopping event. Business interruption coverage can also kick in to cover the lost revenue during the repair period.

I bake from home under Ohio cottage food law — do I need insurance?

Yes. Ohio's cottage food law allows you to sell certain homemade baked goods — but it doesn't protect you from liability. Your homeowner's policy explicitly excludes business operations conducted from your home. If a customer has an allergic reaction to something you baked and sold, your homeowner's policy won't cover the claim. Cottage bakery coverage is a specific product designed for home-based food businesses — and it's far more affordable than most cottage bakers expect.

I make wedding cakes — what happens if the cake is damaged during delivery?

Damage to a wedding cake during delivery is a combination of a product loss and a professional liability matter. If you're delivering in your own vehicle, commercial auto coverage applies to the vehicle incident. The cost of replacing or repairing the cake — and any claims from the client for the impact on their wedding — falls under professional liability. For wedding cake bakeries, professional liability and commercial auto are both essential coverages.
