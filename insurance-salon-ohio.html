WHY SALON INSURANCE IS DIFFERENT

Chemical services and professional tools create liability that standard policies don't cover.

Nail salons and hair salons provide professional services using chemicals, tools, and techniques that carry real liability exposure. A chemical burn from color or relaxer. A nail infection from improper sanitation. An allergic reaction to acrylics or gel products. These are professional liability claims — not General Liability claims — and they're excluded from most standard business policies.

The salon industry also has specific chemical exposure risks for employees — nail dust, fumes, and repeated contact with chemicals create occupational health risks that make Workers Compensation essential for any salon with employed technicians.

Myers Insurance Group builds Ohio salon policies that address both the customer-facing professional liability exposure and the employee-facing occupational health risks — giving you complete coverage from the front door to the back room.

Coverage gaps in most Ohio salon policies

No professional liability. Chemical burns, infections, allergic reactions, and hair damage claims are professional liability matters — not GL claims. Most salon BOPs don't include professional liability by default.

Chemical exposure Workers Comp gaps. Nail technicians face significant chemical exposure risk from acrylics, gels, and cleaning agents. Workers Comp must specifically cover occupational illness — not just physical injuries.

Booth and suite renter gaps. Independent stylists renting booths or suites in your salon should carry their own professional liability. Your policy likely doesn't cover their work — and you could still be named in a claim.

Underinsured equipment. Salon chairs, dryers, color equipment, nail stations, and UV lamps represent significant value — often underinsured on standard property policies.

WHAT WE COVER

Nail salon and hair salon coverage in Ohio.

A complete Ohio salon insurance program covers your services, your space, your equipment, and your team.

General Liability

Covers slip-and-fall, property damage, and non-professional customer injury. Salons are high-traffic environments with wet floors and chemical spills — GL is the foundation of any salon policy.

Professional Liability

Covers claims from services — chemical burns, allergic reactions, infections, nail damage, hair damage. Your most important coverage as a salon — and the one most often missing from standard policies.

Business Property

Covers salon chairs, dryers, nail stations, UV lamps, color equipment, retail product inventory, and build-out against fire, theft, vandalism, and covered perils.

Workers Compensation

Required in Ohio for employees. Chemical exposure, repetitive motion injuries, and slip-and-fall behind the chair are all real occupational risks. Must cover both physical injury and chemical exposure illness.

Business Owner Policy (BOP)

Bundles GL and property for cost-effective base coverage. A BOP is the right starting point for Ohio salons — with professional liability added as an essential endorsement.

Booth & Suite Renter Coverage

Independent stylists and nail techs renting space in your salon should carry their own GL and professional liability. We help salon owners set coverage requirements and protect themselves from renter liability exposure.

Business Interruption

Covers lost revenue when a fire or covered loss forces you to close temporarily. A salon with empty chairs is a salon losing money — BI coverage keeps you financially stable during repairs.

Product Liability

If you sell retail products — nail polish, shampoo, treatments — and a product causes an adverse reaction, product liability covers the claim. Important for any salon with a retail component.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Salon insurance in Ohio — what you need to know.

A client had an allergic reaction to nail products — is that covered?

This is a professional liability claim — specifically a product reaction claim arising from your services. Standard General Liability policies exclude professional services liability. Without professional liability coverage, you're personally responsible for the client's medical expenses, lost wages, and any legal costs. Professional liability is the coverage that responds to this type of claim and is essential for any nail salon offering acrylic, gel, or dip services.

A client claims my stylist damaged their hair with a chemical service — am I covered?

Hair damage from color, bleach, relaxer, or perm services is a professional liability claim. Your GL policy won't cover it. Professional liability covers claims from the actual services your stylists provide — including chemical damage, breakage, and scalp injuries from professional products. This coverage is non-negotiable for any Ohio hair salon offering chemical services.

I rent booths to independent stylists — does my policy cover their work?

Your policy covers the salon premises and your own services — not the work of independent booth renters. Each renter should carry their own General Liability and Professional Liability coverage. As the salon owner, you should require proof of insurance from every booth renter annually and include coverage requirements in your booth rental agreement. We help Ohio salon owners structure their requirements correctly.

Does Ohio require salons to carry insurance?

Ohio State Board of Cosmetology licensing requirements include insurance provisions for licensed establishments. Beyond licensing, your landlord almost certainly requires minimum GL coverage as a condition of your lease. And practically speaking, one uninsured professional liability claim from a chemical service gone wrong can cost far more than years of premiums.
