WHY FLORIST INSURANCE IS DIFFERENT

Florists face risks that most retail businesses never think about.

Florists operate at the intersection of retail, professional services, perishable inventory, and delivery logistics. Your inventory walks out the door after a few days whether you sell it or not. Your refrigeration equipment failing overnight can wipe out thousands of dollars of product. Your delivery van is a commercial vehicle that needs commercial coverage. And the wedding you're doing the flowers for has a contract — and expectations — that create real liability if something goes wrong.

Event florists face particular exposure. A wedding or corporate event where the flowers don't arrive, are the wrong colors, or wilt before the ceremony can generate a professional liability claim that exceeds the value of the contract. Professional liability coverage protects you from claims that arise from the professional services you provide — not just the retail sale of flowers.

Myers Insurance Group builds Ohio florist policies that cover the shop, the coolers, the van, and the events — completely.

The gaps that catch Ohio florists off guard

No spoilage coverage. When your walk-in cooler fails and you lose $5,000 in perishable inventory, standard property policies don't cover spoilage. Dedicated spoilage coverage is essential for any florist.

Delivery vehicles need commercial auto. Personal auto policies exclude delivery use. Every delivery your driver makes in an uninsured vehicle is a potential uninsured accident waiting to happen.

No professional liability for events. A wedding where your flowers are late, wrong, or inadequate is a professional liability claim. GL doesn't cover it — professional liability does.

Underinsured equipment. Walk-in coolers are expensive to repair or replace. Equipment breakdown coverage is critical for any florist whose entire inventory depends on refrigeration.

WHAT WE COVER

Florist and flower shop coverage in Ohio.

A complete Ohio florist insurance program covers your shop, your inventory, your deliveries, and your events.

General Liability

Covers slip-and-fall, customer injury, and property damage in your shop. The foundation of any florist policy — and required by most commercial landlords.

Commercial Property

Covers your shop build-out, display cases, furniture, non-perishable inventory, tools, and equipment against fire, theft, vandalism, and covered perils.

Spoilage Coverage

Covers perishable floral inventory lost due to refrigeration equipment failure. When your walk-in cooler goes down overnight, spoilage coverage pays for the lost inventory. Non-negotiable for any florist with significant floral stock.

Equipment Breakdown

Covers the cost of repairing or replacing your walk-in cooler, refrigeration units, and other mechanical equipment when they fail. Standard property policies exclude mechanical breakdown.

Commercial Auto

Required for all delivery vehicles. Personal auto policies exclude commercial delivery use — every delivery your driver makes needs commercial auto coverage. Covers liability, collision, and comprehensive for your delivery van.

Professional Liability

Covers claims from event services — weddings, corporate events, funerals. If your flowers are late, wrong, or unsatisfactory for a contracted event, professional liability covers the dispute and any resulting claim.

Workers Compensation

Required in Ohio for employees. Floral work involves sharp tools, heavy lifting, chemical exposure from fertilizers and pesticides, and repetitive motion — Workers Comp is essential for any florist with staff.

Business Interruption

Covers lost revenue when a fire or covered loss forces you to close temporarily. For a florist, closure during wedding season or the holiday period can be financially devastating without income replacement.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Florist insurance in Ohio — what you need to know.

My walk-in cooler failed overnight and I lost all my inventory — is that covered?

Only if you have spoilage coverage and equipment breakdown coverage — two separate coverages that standard property policies typically exclude. Spoilage coverage pays for the lost perishable inventory. Equipment breakdown coverage pays to repair or replace the cooler itself. Without both, the full cost of the inventory loss and the repair comes out of your pocket. For any florist with a significant floral inventory, these two coverages are non-negotiable.

I'm doing the flowers for a wedding and the client is threatening to sue because she didn't like the arrangements — am I covered?

This is a professional liability claim. General Liability covers premises injury and property damage — not disputes about the quality or delivery of your professional services. Professional liability covers claims arising from your event work — including failure to deliver as contracted, incorrect flowers, late delivery, and quality disputes. For event florists who take on wedding and corporate contracts, professional liability is essential coverage.

My delivery driver had an accident in the shop van — are we covered?

Yes — if you have commercial auto coverage. Personal auto policies exclude vehicles used for business delivery purposes. Your delivery van is a commercial vehicle and needs a commercial auto policy that covers liability, collision, and comprehensive. One accident in an uninsured delivery vehicle can expose your business to significant uninsured liability.

Do I need insurance to do flowers for weddings and events?

Most venues and event coordinators now require vendors — including florists — to carry minimum General Liability coverage and provide a certificate of insurance before they'll allow you to work an event. Beyond the venue requirement, wedding and event contracts create professional liability exposure that makes proper coverage essential for any Ohio florist taking on event work.
