What most people don't know about their policy

Your homeowner's policy probably doesn't cover flood damage. Flood insurance is a separate policy — and most Ohio homeowners don't have it.

Your auto policy may not cover you for business use. If you drive for Uber, deliver food, or use your car for work — you may have a gap.

Your life insurance through work disappears when you leave. Employer-provided life insurance isn't portable. You need your own policy.

Minimum liability limits aren't enough. Ohio's minimum auto liability limits are among the lowest in the country. One serious accident can exceed them.
