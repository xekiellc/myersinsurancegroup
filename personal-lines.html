WHAT WE COVER

Personal coverage for
every stage of your life.

From your first apartment to your retirement home — Myers Insurance Group builds personal coverage programs that actually protect what you have.

Homeowners Insurance

Covers your home, your belongings, and your liability. Make sure you have replacement cost coverage — not actual cash value — or you'll be underinsured when it matters most.

Auto Insurance

Ohio minimum limits are dangerously low. One serious accident can generate claims that exceed minimum coverage by tens of thousands of dollars. We build auto policies with adequate limits.

Life Insurance

Term, whole, and universal life options. If someone depends on your income, life insurance is not optional. It costs less than you think — and the conversation you're putting off is overdue.

Umbrella Insurance

Adds $1M+ of liability protection above your home and auto limits. At roughly $200-400 per year, it's one of the best values in personal insurance — and most people don't have it.

Renters Insurance

Your landlord's policy covers the building — not your belongings. Renters insurance protects your personal property and provides liability coverage, typically for under $20 per month.

Identity Theft Protection

Covers the cost of recovering from identity theft — legal fees, lost wages, and restoration services. Identity theft is the fastest-growing crime in the US. Most people are unprotected.

COMMON GAPS

What most Ohio families
are missing.

These are the coverage gaps we find most often when we review personal insurance policies.

No flood insurance

Standard homeowners policies never cover floods. Flood insurance is a separate policy — and most Ohio homeowners don't have it. If you've been assuming you're covered, you have a serious gap.

Ohio minimum auto limits

Ohio's minimum auto liability is 25/50/25. A serious car accident today can easily generate $500,000+ in medical claims. Minimum limits leave you personally liable for the difference.

No uninsured motorist coverage

Ohio has some of the highest rates of uninsured drivers in the country. Without UM/UIM coverage, if an uninsured driver seriously injures you, you pay out of pocket for your own medical bills.

Rideshare coverage gap

If you drive for Uber, Lyft, DoorDash, or any delivery service, your personal auto policy excludes coverage during that activity. One accident while working and your claim gets denied.

Employer-only life insurance

Employer life insurance disappears the day you leave — by choice, layoff, or disability. A personal policy protects your family regardless of your employment status.
