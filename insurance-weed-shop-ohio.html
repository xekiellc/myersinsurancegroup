WHY CANNABIS INSURANCE IS DIFFERENT

Standard insurance doesn't work for cannabis businesses.

Ohio's cannabis industry is growing fast — and so are the insurance challenges that come with it. Most standard commercial insurance carriers either exclude cannabis operations entirely or offer policies that are dangerously inadequate for the unique risks dispensaries face.

Cannabis businesses deal with high cash volumes, valuable inventory, regulatory compliance requirements, and product liability exposure that simply doesn't exist in other retail environments. Your insurance needs to reflect that reality.

Myers Insurance Group works with specialty carriers that understand the cannabis market and write real coverage for Ohio dispensaries — from single-location operations to multi-site portfolios.

The cannabis insurance problem in Ohio

Most standard business insurance excludes cannabis. Walk into a State Farm or Allstate agency with a dispensary — they'll turn you away.

Your inventory isn't covered by standard property policies. Cannabis product has to be specifically scheduled on a specialty policy.

Cash handling exposure is enormous. Federal banking restrictions mean many dispensaries operate heavily in cash — which requires specific theft and burglary coverage.

WHAT WE COVER

Cannabis dispensary coverage in Ohio.

Every dispensary is different. Here are the core coverages we build Ohio cannabis policies around.

General Liability

Covers customer injury, slip-and-fall, property damage, and third-party claims. The foundation of any dispensary policy — and it must be written on a cannabis-friendly form.

Product Liability

Covers claims from contaminated, mislabeled, or defective cannabis products. This is your most critical coverage — one product liability claim can end an uninsured dispensary.

Commercial Property

Covers your build-out, displays, security equipment, safes, and non-cannabis inventory against fire, theft, vandalism, and covered perils.

Crop & Inventory Coverage

Covers your cannabis product inventory — the coverage that standard property policies specifically exclude. Protects against theft, spoilage, and loss of your actual product.

Theft & Burglary

High cash volumes and valuable inventory make dispensaries prime theft targets. Dedicated theft coverage including vault, safe, and cash-in-transit protection.

Employee Dishonesty

Covers theft by employees — a significant risk in any cash-heavy retail environment. Internal theft is one of the most common losses in cannabis retail.

Workers Compensation

Required in Ohio for most employers. Covers employee injuries on the job — including those involving cannabis handling, equipment, and security operations.

Cyber Liability

Point-of-sale systems, customer databases, and compliance tracking systems all create cyber exposure. Data breaches in regulated industries carry significant notification and legal costs.

Professional Liability

Covers claims from cannabis recommendations or advice given by your staff. As dispensary staff take on more of an advisory role, professional liability becomes increasingly important.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Cannabis insurance in Ohio — what you need to know.

Can I get standard business insurance for my Ohio dispensary?

Not from most carriers. The majority of standard commercial insurers either exclude cannabis entirely or offer policies that don't cover your actual cannabis inventory and product liability. You need a specialty cannabis insurance carrier — and we know which ones write quality Ohio policies.

Does my cannabis policy cover my actual product inventory?

Standard commercial property policies specifically exclude cannabis product. You need crop and inventory coverage written on a cannabis-specific policy form. This is one of the most important and most overlooked gaps for Ohio dispensaries.

What happens if a customer claims my product made them sick?

That's a product liability claim — and it's one of the most serious exposures for any dispensary. Without product liability coverage written on a cannabis-friendly form, you're personally liable for the full cost of any contamination or adverse reaction claim.

Does Ohio require dispensaries to carry insurance?

Ohio's cannabis licensing requirements include insurance mandates. Dispensary licenses require minimum liability coverage as part of the application and renewal process. Operating without adequate coverage puts your license at risk in addition to your financial exposure.
